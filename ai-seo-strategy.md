## Competitive Playbook: 5 AI-Driven Tactics

### 1. Topical Authority Mapping
Use MarketMuse or Semrush to map every subtopic in your niche. Build content clusters that cover topics more comprehensively than any single competitor.

### 2. SERP Feature Hijacking
Use Frase to analyze which SERP features (featured snippets, PAA, knowledge panels) competitors hold. Reverse-engineer the content format and structure, then optimize with Surfer SEO.

### 3. Content Decay Detection
Use Semrush or Ahrefs to identify your pages losing rankings. Feed declining pages into Clearscope to refresh them with updated semantic coverage before competitors overtake you.

### 4. Programmatic SEO with AI
Use Claude API or GPT to generate hundreds of long-tail landing pages (e.g., "[tool] vs [competitor]", "[topic] in [city]") at scale with unique, high-quality content. Validate quality with Surfer SEO scores.

### 5. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
Optimize content to be cited by AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews):
- Add clear, quotable definitions and statistics
- Use structured data (FAQ, HowTo schema)
- Include authoritative citations and original data
- Write concise, direct answers in the first paragraph
