📋 Community rules
1
Share real results. Post actual log screenshots if you're claiming P&L. No fake numbers. We verify when it seems off.
2
No financial advice. Share your experience, not instructions. "Here's what I did" not "you should do this." We're all responsible for our own trades.
3
No shill posts. Promoting other courses, bots, signals services, or anything you personally profit from will get you banned immediately.
4
Keep it on topic. Off-topic goes in #off-topic. Market discussion in #market-news. Bot questions in #setup-help. It keeps things usable.
5
Never share private keys. Not in any channel. Not in DMs. If you see anyone asking for private keys, report it immediately — it's a scam.
