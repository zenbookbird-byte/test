Questions
Stuff people ask before buying
Do I need to know how to code? +
No. Module 3 is a complete screen-recorded walkthrough — installing Python, setting up your .env file, funding your Polygon wallet. I go slow and I don't skip steps. If you can follow a recipe, you can do this.
How much money do I need to start? +
The bot defaults to paper trading with a fake $50 budget so you can watch it work before risking anything. For live trading, I'd recommend $100–$500 USDC to start. Kelly sizing means it works sensibly at any budget — small or large.
Is the bot actually fully automated? +
Yes. Once it's running it polls wallet activity every 15 seconds, detects signals, places orders, and logs everything automatically. Most of my students check their logs once a day, that's it. Module 7 shows you how to put it on a cheap VPS so it runs even when your laptop is off.
What markets does the bot trade? +
By default it focuses on geopolitical and war markets — that's where the sharpest wallets are most active. Module 6 shows you how to extend it to BTC, ETH, and SOL crypto price prediction markets. You can run both at the same time.
What exactly happens in the Private Elite sessions? +
We meet on Zoom 4 times. I share my screen, walk through real trades I'm placing with my own money, and answer everything you've got. I also review your specific bot config — pull it up live and adjust it with you watching. You keep the recordings of every session.
Can I upgrade from a lower tier later? +
Yes, always. If you start on Starter and decide you want the bot code, you just pay the difference to upgrade to Pro + Bot. Same for moving to Private Elite. Email me, I handle it manually within 24 hours.
What's the refund policy? +
30 days, no questions asked. If you go through the course, set up the bot, run it in paper mode, and decide it's not for you — email me and I'll refund you in full. I've done fewer than 10 refunds across 2,400+ students. The guarantee exists because I'm confident in the product, not because I expect to use it much.
Is Polymarket available in my country? +
US residents are currently restricted from Polymarket. Most other countries are fine. It's a decentralised protocol on Polygon so there's no central sign-up — but always check your local regulations before trading. This course is educational. Do your own research before putting in money.
Will the bot still work if lots of people use it? +
Good question. The edge comes from copying a specific set of wallets, not from a signal everyone acts on at once. Each student has a small individual position relative to the market size — we're not moving prices. I keep student numbers in mind and won't sell this to 100,000 people.
